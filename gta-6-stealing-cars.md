In GTA 6, carjacking is not just pressing a button near a car. Vehicles have alarms and onboard security. GameSpot's tips guide explains the basics ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-tips-everything-i-wish-i-knew-before-i-started-playing/)).

## Check the car first with Waink

Waink is an in-game app you can open straight away by holding up on the D-pad. Highlight a vehicle with Waink open and it shows the security system, the sell cost and the registration cost.

## What each security type does

| Security | Effect |
|---|---|
| Alarm | Smashing the window alerts nearby NPCs. |
| Standard tracker | The car cannot be modified until it has been registered. |
| Advanced tracker | The car pings the cops if it is moved too far from its original location. GameSpot says the only way to stop the ping is to drive fast. |
| Keyless (high-end) | Needs a key cloner to start. Most other vehicles can be hotwired. |

These layers limit which cars you can take and own in the early hours. More carjacking tools unlock as you progress.

## Keeping a car

If you find a car you want to keep, take it to Pay 'n' Spray and pay the registration fee. The worse the car is, the cheaper the fee. Once a car is registered you can call it at any time with the Scooter Bros app.

A stolen car also counts as a clue. See [wanted levels and Heat icons]({{ site.baseurl }}/gta-6-wanted-levels-heat-explained/) for how a vehicle becomes Heat and how to clear it.
