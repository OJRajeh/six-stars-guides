In GTA 6 the police track you by clues, shown as Heat icons. GameSpot's advice is that the quickest way to lose the cops is to deal with those heat indicators as early as you can ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-tips-everything-i-wish-i-knew-before-i-started-playing/)). Clear all of them without the police seeing you do it, and they will not be able to find you.

## The clue checklist

- **Outfit.** Wear a hat and bandana for the crime and take them off after. Keep a spare outfit in the trunk, or buy one at a clothing store.
- **Weapons.** Put your pistol away and stow or drop long guns. GameSpot says dropped weapons you own return to your trunk.
- **Vehicle.** Ditch the car or change vehicles.
- **Evidence.** If you robbed a shop, destroy the camera footage in the back room.
- **Partner.** Split from your partner so they are not a clue either.

GamesRadar's Heat page explains the six Heat icons. Hiding weapons or changing clothes clears that heat right away, and the accomplice icon clears when you split up. If either character is caught, you are still busted ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-wanted-levels-heat/)).

## A simple routine

1. Before the job, put on your disguise and check your weapon is the one you want.
2. During the job, note where the cameras are.
3. After the job, get out of sight and clear the clues from the list above, one at a time.
4. Check that no icon is left before you stop running.
