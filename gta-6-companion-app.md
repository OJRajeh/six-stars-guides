*Last checked November 25, 2026.*

**Short answer:** as of today, no official GTA 6 companion app from Rockstar has turned up in the sources below. Techzei checked Rockstar's App Store and Google Play listings and the official GTA VI site on November 20 and found none ([Techzei](https://techzei.com/gta-6-companion-app/)). Leonida Explorer reached the same answer, reviewed November 22 ([Leonida Explorer](https://leonidaexplorer.com/gta-6-companion-app)). The phones you use inside GTA 6 are in-game devices, not an app you download to your own phone.

**Disclosure:** we make Six Stars, one of the unofficial companion apps discussed on this page. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

## Be careful with anything labelled "official"

Malwarebytes has warned about GTA 6 scams that can cost you an account or money ([Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/scams/2026/11/gta-6-scams-could-cost-you-your-account-money-or-crypto)). BforeAI has reported unauthorized GTA 6 apps on Google Play ([Business Computing Journal](https://businesscomputingjournal.com/bforeai-gta-6-unauthorized-apps-google-play/)). Before you install anything, check the developer name, read what the listing says about affiliation, and look at whether the app names its sources.

## What your phone can already do

On PS5, the PlayStation app and PS Remote Play handle remote downloads and streaming from your own console. On Xbox, the Xbox app does the same. These control your console. They do not explain the game.

## How to pick an unofficial companion app

Fan-made apps differ a lot. Some are map viewers, some are cheat lists, some are wikis. Use this checklist:

1. **Who made it?** A named developer and a privacy policy are the minimum.
2. **Does it credit sources?** Cheat codes and tips spread fast and are often wrong. Dated, named sources let you judge them.
3. **Can you avoid spoilers?** GTA 6 is a story game. A spoiler setting matters.
4. **What does it cost, and for what?** Check the in-app purchases before you rely on a feature.
5. **Does it say it is unofficial?** An honest listing will.
