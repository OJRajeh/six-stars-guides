GTA 6 changed how the police work. Your wanted level now goes up to six stars, and the stars look different depending on what the cops know. A second system, Heat, shows which clues they have on you. GamesRadar's guide to both is the source for this page ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-wanted-levels-heat/), updated November 23).

## What the four star types mean

| Star colour | What it means |
|---|---|
| Black and white | The cops are investigating a crime but do not know who you are. Avoid attention and you can slip away. |
| Gray | They know information about you, based on your Heat icons, and are actively searching. |
| White | They are chasing you and have you in sight. |
| Red | You have evaded them, but they still have information on you. Lose the Heat icons before they identify you again. |

On the map and compass, an area under investigation shows as a blue radial. When you are being chased, it turns into a red circle, and the compass shows it too. Head away from the red line and you will eventually be outside it.

## The six Heat icons

Heat icons are red and sit in the top-right corner. They show the clues the police are using, taken from witnesses and from the police's own observations.

1. Security camera footage
2. Accomplice
3. Clothing
4. Face
5. Weapon
6. Vehicle

The more icons you have active, the harder it is to lose the cops.

## How each clue is cleared

- **Weapon and clothing.** Hiding your weapon or changing clothes removes that Heat instantly.
- **Vehicle.** After a car chase, the vehicle icon clears as soon as you step out of the car.
- **Accomplice.** Split up Jason and Lucia. If either one is caught while you are apart, you are still busted.
- **Face and cameras.** GamesRadar suggests prevention: wear a mask or face covering, and avoid or take out security cameras. Wiping footage can work, but some cameras have a cloud backup timer.

Heat also shows on the map by area. Hover over a tinted zone to see which icons you have active there. They fade after a while, except in certain story-mandated zones.
