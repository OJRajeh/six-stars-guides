GTA 6 does not let you open the map and warp to a point. GameSpot's tips guide lists several other ways to cross Leonida quickly ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-tips-everything-i-wish-i-knew-before-i-started-playing/)).

| Method | What it costs | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| RydeMe | Money. For an extra $100 you can skip straight to the destination. | Expensive early on. You need to unlock the app first. |
| Buses and trains | Cheap | They run on a schedule, so you wait. GameSpot says the network is more convenient in Vice City than outside it, but a train system extends across all of Leonida. |
| Switch characters | Free | The character you are not playing as usually hangs out near your current safehouse, so it works well when you have gone far from home. |

## How RydeMe works

Once you unlock the app, mark a spot on your map, call a car and get in the back seat. The $100 option skips the ride and puts you at the destination.

## When to use what

- **Short on cash:** use buses and trains, and accept the wait.
- **Far from your safehouse with the other character waiting there:** switch characters.
- **Cash to spare and no patience:** pay for the RydeMe skip.
