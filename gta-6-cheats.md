## How GTA 6 cheats work

GTA 6 locks its cheats behind the story. Once they unlock, a Cheats option appears in the Progress tab of the main menu, and each cheat has its own unlock challenge. GamesRadar reports 61 cheats in total. This page lists the 18 that outlets had reported in full as of November 24, 2026.

In free roam, press Up on the D-pad to pull out your phone, hold L1/LB and R1/RB, and enter the code on the D-pad.

**Warning:** saving is disabled while cheats are active, and trophies and achievements stay off for the rest of the session. Make a clean save first.
